Documentation, coverage, generated-conflict repair, release helpers and their
verification tests are adapted from [Ownerloom at 273edcbe](https://github.com/astrivant/ownerloom/tree/273edcbe25d55c3e9667d2fcf93921a42d0ffd6b/scripts).
That revision records their earlier Ignorecraft and Polyad provenance.
The retained [BSL 1.1 terms](Polyad.LICENSE) apply to these development helpers,
which are excluded from Argivore distributions.

The Markdown math and Mermaid checker retains its
[Apache-2.0 notice](documentation/markdown-checks.LICENSE) from the
[1Password Agent Broker](https://github.com/astrivant/1password-agent-broker/tree/ed67910789db3c0b26e2ffc0fe78f42d5585d828/scripts).
JavaScript dependencies are installed through their committed npm lockfile.
